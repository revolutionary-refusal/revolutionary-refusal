## Financial custody crosscheck
44 CFR 206.44(a)–(c) describes the FEMA-State agreement, its assistance terms, and executed amendments; exceptions allow essential emergency services or specified housing assistance before signature. Subsection (d) allows variation for a modified federal emergency. Iowa's 20 November announcement establishes activation of the state emergency operations center, not the execution of an agreement. FEMA's training guidance distinguishes federal operations support (fully federally funded) from direct federal assistance (subject to the declaration's cost-share terms). It also describes the mission assignment as a funding and work authorization, not proof of disbursement. Do not apply a uniform 75/25 cost split to the entire incident or assume FEMA funded Army statutory responsibilities.
