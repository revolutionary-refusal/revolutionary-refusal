# IAAAP — airborne lead nondetection: independent method crosscheck
27 November 2026

EPA's 26 November release reports continued preliminary airborne lead nondetections; post-event laboratory results were anticipated as early as 27 November, not guaranteed. On this check the EPA incident register still listed eight documents, the dashboard supplied no readable observations, and the Illinois state page was last updated 25 November. No new validated concentrations retrieved.

New evidence: EPA/Battelle's September 2012 Environmental Technology Verification report EPA/600/R-12/680, printed p.14, table 5-1 (visually checked), describes historical Xact 625 testing at Marietta, Ohio. For lead, the reported normal hourly quantitation limit was 1.54 ng/m³; daily quantitation limit 1.60 ng/m³; comparison laboratory ICP/MS detection limit 0.07 ng/m³. The daily value reconciles as (22×1.54 + 2.18 + 0.5×3.09)/23.5 = 1.6002 ng/m³ (rounded 1.60). These are different metrics and sampling conditions, not interchangeable thresholds or Iowa measurements. The Iowa press release names Xact monitors but does not establish the precise model, settings, or detection/reporting limit. A 2012 study cannot determine Iowa instrument performance. The verification report also documents good agreement for lead overall and possible positive bias associated with blank filter tape, not proof of false nondetections.

PROVEN: published historical instrument verification, EPA's preliminary statement and the accessible register count. SUSPECTED: a dashboard zero may represent a reporting convention rather than literal absence; actual coding unknown. UNVERIFIED: Iowa model, instrument-specific limits, quality flags, validated concentrations, completeness, and independent lab confirmation.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7 and Army environmental custodian, separately, for 24–27 November 2026: releasable aggregate instrument model/method, sampling and analysis intervals, detection and quantitation limits, blank correction, calibration acceptance, invalid/missing observations, treatment of nondetects, dashboard data dictionary and revision log, and paired independent laboratory results with qualifiers. State environmental agencies: independently retained copies and analytical review. No private locations or sensitive facility details requested. Request is not submitted.
