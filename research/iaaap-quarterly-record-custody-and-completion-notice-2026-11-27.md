# IAAAP — quarterly record custody and completion notice
27 November 2026 | Public accountability research; not a safety advisory.

## New documentary findings
The historical Army–EPA Federal Facility Agreement, docket VII-F-90-0029, filed 21 September 1990 (indexed as 1991), provides a second documentary route beyond the emergency permit. Section XXVII.F (printed pp. 61–62) requires an indexed administrative record at or near the site, copies of included documents to EPA, and updates and index copies at least quarterly. EPA decides whether material belongs in the record. Section IX.D.2–4 (printed p. 27) distinguishes advance notice for emergency removals from nonemergency removal consultation, and calls for written completion notification and a description of the removal. The current status, amendments, and applicability of these historical terms to the 2026 RCRA emergency response are unverified. Destruction of the hazardous inventory is not necessarily the completion of environmental cleanup.

The installation's CERCLA restoration portal lists an October 2026 index and explicitly warns that some downloadable records omit laboratory data packages due to file size. This is evidence of a disclosed online-access limitation, not evidence that data have been destroyed. The portal and EPA incident register are different scopes and not interchangeable. The latter still lists eight items; EPA's 26 November release remains the latest primary environmental sampling update retrieved; no newly validated laboratory concentrations found in this check.

## Evidence and counterevidence
PROVEN: historical text and the portal's stated limitations. SUSPECTED: a public-only archive may be incomplete. UNVERIFIED: current FFA scope, transmission receipts, actual completion date, laboratory results, procurement cancellation disposition, and costs. Previous 25 November FFA and administrative-record notes examined; this addendum targets additional clauses, not a new finding of misconduct. Copies held by separate custodians can verify transmission and completeness but do not constitute independent sampling. Emergency permitting and CERCLA records may legitimately differ. The Army's coordination with EPA and favorable preliminary air monitoring are counterevidence to assumptions of non-notification or contamination.

## Unsent records scope
Separately request from Army environmental office and EPA Region 7: current FFA and amendments, 2026 quarterly index transmittals and receipts, applicability determination for the November response, any written removal completion notice, and complete releasable laboratory packages associated with the relevant records, with versions and qualifiers. Request aggregate, non-sensitive information, exclude private and sensitive operational details. Ask for a dated explanation if an item is not applicable or not yet created. No request submitted.
