# IAAAP — administrative record access window and permittee distinction
26 November 2026

EPA's 3 November 2026 public notice for the third storage-only emergency permit (issued 29 September; stated end 26 December) offers a 45-day administrative record review at Burlington Public Library and EPA Region 7 Records Center. If counted from publication, day 45 falls 17 December inclusive or 18 December exclusive. This is a preservation checkpoint, not a confirmed legal deadline: the notice does not specify the exact last day. The separate 16 November notice for the 13 November storage-and-treatment permit (stated end 11 February 2027) says another notice will announce administrative record availability. No such separate notice was retrieved in this check. The EPA response register lists eight documents; that list is not proof of a complete administrative record.

A distinct historical EPA notice dated 20 April 2020 for an emergency permit issued 2 March 2020 names BOTH the Army and American Ordnance LLC as permittees, whereas the November 2026 notice names the Army alone. This is documentary evidence of differing permittee scopes, not proof that the contractor has been released from other duties or that the 2020 event caused the current incident. The 2020 notice also identifies the same two public record review locations and a 45-day review window.

PROVEN: published notice dates, named permittees, stated review locations and durations. UNVERIFIED: precise access cutoff, complete index, any supersession of the September permit, 2026 contractor allocation, validated environmental results. SUSPECTED: risk of losing convenient access to the earlier administrative record if the review window closes. No misconduct or withholding established. EPA's 26 November environmental update remains the latest retrieved primary update.

Unsent records scope: request the complete public/redacted index for each 2026 emergency permit, exact opening/closing dates, any supersession decision, publication receipts, and the basis for permittee designation; separately request the Army's non-sensitive allocation of environmental responsibilities and the library's public record inventory. No requests sent. Preserve separate copies from EPA and library when accessible; neither a link list nor this note is an original archive.
