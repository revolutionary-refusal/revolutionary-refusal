# IAAAP — contract cost responsibility and governance, 2026-11-25

## New primary evidence
GAO-23-105352 (October 13, 2022), printed p. 40, identifies American Ordnance as vendor since October 2008 and records the original facility operation/maintenance contract value at $427.8 million, with potential extension through December 2033. The rendered original page confirms the decimal; extracted text incorrectly presents $4278 million. The contract was described as no-cost, consistent with GAO-11-488R (April 1, 2011), which explains the exchange of government asset use for operating and maintenance services. The original value is not a cash payment or incident liability. GAO also reports $143.2 million FY2021 production-base-support funding and $1.4 million FY2021 ARMS revenue from 19 tenants. These are distinct historical categories, not a net cost or current incident total.

GAO-23-105352 p. 25, table 5, confirms the Iowa performance work statement describes maintenance types, with the government resolving terminology disputes. Contractor-funded maintenance and predominantly government-funded modernization are generally distinguished; actual 2026 waste treatment and cleanup classification must be verified from the operative contract and changes, not inferred from general maintenance terms.

GAO's current recommendation register: recommendation 1 (clarify governing roles) OPEN. February 2026 Army report attributed delay to reorganization; May 2026 update anticipated completion later in calendar 2026. Recommendation 2 (lessons-learned mechanism) CLOSED–IMPLEMENTED in August 2024. As of November 25, 2026, this register does not establish current governance revision or missed year-end deadline.

Printed pp. 15–16 describe historical responsibility: Joint Munitions Command installation commander for environmental and safety concerns, administrative contracting officer for compliance and payment approval, Army Contracting Command procuring contracting officer for changing terms. Current delegations remain unverified. No finding of incident causation or individual or corporate misconduct.
