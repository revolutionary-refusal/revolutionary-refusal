# IAAAP — dashboard coverage and environmental data custody

26 November 2026. EPA response site 17211 now reports that its public dashboard includes locations for dust wipes, soil, tap water and surface-water samples. This is an expansion of published coverage beyond the November 25 dashboard launch; it does not establish that analytical results are published or validated. The linked ArcGIS application returned no readable data rows in this review. EPA's separate November 26 release reports favorable real-time airborne lead nondetections and anticipates initial laboratory results as early as November 27. Illinois's update links to the same federal dashboard, not an independent measurement system. The EPA document register still lists eight documents.

PROVEN: these dated agency statements and public register entries. SUSPECTED: a single-dashboard dependency could impede reproducibility; no finding of suppression. UNVERIFIED: sample-level results, validation flags, detection limits, coverage completeness and independent laboratory confirmation.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7 and Army separately, plus state environmental authorities, for dated, machine-readable public sample tables with anonymized identifiers, media, sampling dates, units, reporting limits, validation qualifiers, corrections, aggregate coverage counts, and version history; request preservation of underlying records and independent laboratory reports.
