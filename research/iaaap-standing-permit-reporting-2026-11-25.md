## New findings
EPA's final September 2018 hazardous waste management permit identifies the Army and American Ordnance LLC as co-permittees, unlike the November 13, 2026 emergency permit naming the Army. The standing permit, conditions II.E.14–17 (printed pages 27–28), calls for oral notification within 24 hours of awareness of qualifying noncompliance that may endanger health or the environment, written follow-up within five days (or 15 days if the Director grants the waiver), other reportable noncompliance within 30 days of occurrence, and written correction of previously omitted relevant facts or incorrect information within seven days of discovery. The 24-hour and five-day framework is independently corroborated by 40 CFR 270.30(l)(6). The seven-day and 30-day details are permit-specific. Conditions II.E.19–20 (printed pages 28–29) require preservation of underlying data over the permit term including continued/reissued permits and certified signatures on required submissions. These are separate from the November emergency permit reporting deadlines and state air permit requirements.
