# IAAAP — permit-copy integrity check
27 November 2026 | Public records research, not a safety advisory.

EPA site 17211 still lists eight records, with displayed sizes totaling 289,643 KB (previously recorded). The application and QAPP account for 268,591 KB, or 92.73% of that inventory. This is a download-volume concentration, not a content or risk weighting. The two EPA-hosted copies of the November 13 emergency permit were visually compared on PDF pages 1, 25 and 26. The first and last numbered pages agree in appearance; the 26th page is blank in both. A 25-page printed numbering versus 26-page PDF count is thus not evidence of a missing substantive page. Exact byte equality and intermediate page equality remain unverified. Both copies share an agency custodian, not independent archives.

The November 26 EPA release remains the latest primary sampling update retrieved. No new validated lab concentrations or signed contract records obtained. No inference of contamination, concealment or contractor misconduct. Prior quarterly record custody note reviewed.

Records scope (unsent): request from EPA Region 7 and Army separately a versioned public record inventory with byte counts, SHA-256 digests if held, publication dates, redaction/version history, and lab data release status. Request independently held copies from the public record repository when available. Exclude personal and sensitive operational details.
