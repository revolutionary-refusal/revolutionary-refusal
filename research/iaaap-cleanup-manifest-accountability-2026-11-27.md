# IAAAP — cleanup manifest accountability
27 November 2026

EPA's 26 November incident update anticipated laboratory results beginning as early as 27 November. The incident document register still lists eight documents in the present retrieval; no new validated concentrations were obtained. Missouri's advisory attributes its preliminary air finding to EPA, not an independent laboratory result.

For any qualifying off-site hazardous-waste cleanup shipments, EPA's e-Manifest guidance identifies a separate financial and custody trail. Public domestic manifest data are generally available 90 days after receipt, not after the incident. For illustration only, receipt on 25 November 2026 would imply a nominal public-data checkpoint of 23 February 2027; no such receipt is established. Regulators can request corrections, which handlers must make within 30 days under the Third Rule (published 26 July 2024). The system retains correction history, although availability of that history to the general public is not established. Receiving facilities certify records and are charged per submission; state regulatory records and generator copies offer additional corroboration.

EPA's FY2026–27 fee schedule (shipments initiated from 1 October 2025) lists $25 scanned image, $7 data plus image, and $5 electronic/hybrid per manifest. These are administrative fees, not cleanup charges, actual expenditures, or evidence of a shipment. The 5 March 2026 paper sunset rule is a proposal on EPA's current rulemaking page, not a final mandate.

PROVEN: published reporting and fee framework. SUSPECTED: delayed public visibility and reliance on mutable records may complicate retrospective reconciliation. UNVERIFIED: incident-specific waste classification, shipment occurrence, certified records, invoices, payments, and validated environmental results. On-site activity and other exemptions may not require a manifest; absence of public records does not demonstrate noncompliance.

Unsent records scope: request aggregate incident cleanup classification, disposition and acceptance totals, certified manifest counts if applicable, revision/exception log, aggregate invoicing and payments, and reconciliation to Army cleanup records from EPA Region 7, Army and relevant state regulators. Seek independent receiving-facility certification through appropriate official channels; exclude operational details, routes and personal information. No request submitted.
