# IAAAP — court review and emergency exclusion (2026-11-25)

## Newly inspected judicial record
Iowa Environmental Council v. EPA, Eighth Circuit No. 26-3374, filed November 23, 2026, pp. 4, 7–9, 11. Published opinion reproduced by Justia; page 4 and page 11 images inspected. The November 21 EPA notice described an Army intention to invoke a separate RCRA emergency exclusion if the permit was not in effect. Footnote 1 expressly excludes the Army's proposed course of action from the court's decision because the Army is not a defendant. The panel denied the stay and lifted the administrative stay, finding the petitioners had not shown likely success; it did not issue a final merits judgment. The panel considered permit safeguards and the Army's evaluation of alternatives sufficient at this stage. The earlier September 2025 procurement notice does not establish earlier knowledge of the specific degradation found in March 2026. No unlawful invocation of the exclusion is established.
