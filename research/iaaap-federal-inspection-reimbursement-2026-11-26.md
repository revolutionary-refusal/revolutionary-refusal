# IAAAP — federal inspection reimbursement and independent records
26 November 2026 | Public financial and environmental accountability.

Newly identified statutory accounting route: 42 USC 6927(c) (House Office of Law Revision Counsel text through 10 November 2026) requires EPA annual thorough inspections of federally owned or operated hazardous-waste treatment, storage or disposal facilities, and requires the owning/operating federal agency to reimburse EPA for inspection costs. Subsection (a) provides for inspection sample receipts, a split portion upon owner/operator request, and prompt provision of analytical results to the owner/operator. Subsection (b) provides public access subject to applicable confidentiality restrictions; (b)(4) provides a separate route for authorized congressional committees. These are inspection-specific provisions, not an automatic right to samples from all emergency-response monitoring or an unrestricted public disclosure right.

EPA's hazardous-waste cleanup and manifest guidance identifies EPA Region 7 as administering Iowa's RCRA program. The Army and EPA are separate custodians for applicable inspection and reimbursement records. A federal inspection invoice is not a contractor liability determination, penalty or proof of payment; do not combine it with incident response expenses or existing procurement totals.
