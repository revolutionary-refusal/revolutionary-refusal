# IAAAP — water sampling and reporting custody — 2026-11-26

## Newly extracted commitments
EPA's November 26 update reports that the first round of drinking-water sampling occurred November 25, including all drinking-water intakes within the agency's modeled coverage, and a second round was planned for November 26. Soil and dust collection was expected to finish November 26; EPA also reports monitoring volatile organic compounds and particulate matter, and mobilizing 170 pieces of equipment. These are agency-reported activities and plans, not validated laboratory results, verified final completion, or a financial cost. The first results were expected as early as November 27. EPA's response document register still lists eight records on November 26.

## Independent custody and regulatory distinction
Sampling water at an intake is not equivalent to testing finished water or consumer taps. The current eCFR 40 CFR 141.80 sets November 1, 2027 as the general compliance date for revised Lead and Copper Rule requirements, subject to exceptions and transitional provisions. 40 CFR 142.15(b)(3) introduces broad annual state compliance-monitoring data reporting no earlier than May 24, 2027. Existing quarterly reporting includes system-level 90th-percentile lead data. Emergency response samples may not qualify as routine compliance samples; distinguish raw, treated and tap water, and do not assume EPA's emergency dashboard contains all water-utility records.
