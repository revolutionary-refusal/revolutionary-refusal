# IAAAP — sampling count reconciliation
27 November 2026 (observation on 28 November UTC).

EPA's 26 November release reported 1,591 baseline samples, more than 1,000 post-event samples, and no detectable airborne lead in its real-time monitors. It identified Region 7, mobile, and contracted laboratories. On 27 November at 5:30 p.m. (time as displayed), Iowa's News Now attributed updated counts of 1,639 baseline and 1,618 follow-up samples to EPA's Facebook update, along with 187 assessed sites and cessation of air monitoring on 26 November after 30 hours. The original social post was not retrieved. These later figures are secondary-source attribution, not independently verified primary data. Arithmetic: 48 additional baseline records (3.02% relative to 1,591); 21 fewer post-event than baseline records; combined 3,257 reported samples. The post/baseline count ratio 98.72% is not a paired-site completion rate, and later baseline additions can be intake reconciliation rather than additional pre-event collection. Earlier post-event count is a lower-bound statement, not an exact value. No validated concentrations retrieved; EPA incident register still lists eight documents, and the linked dashboard returned no readable data in this retrieval. Nondetection is counterevidence to airborne contamination at monitored points, not proof of universal absence or of soil/water safety.

PROVEN: 26 November EPA release and current document index. UNVERIFIED: later original statement, sampling denominators, laboratory results, and actual cessation of all monitoring versus one monitoring modality. SUSPECTED: no wrongdoing; data reconciliation may explain changed baseline counts.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7, contracted laboratories and state environmental authorities separately: aggregate sampling count revisions and intake cutoffs, de-identified sample pairing and validation status, method detection/reporting limits, monitoring cessation decision and scope, lab receipt dates, independently held results and quality review. Exclude private addresses and sensitive facility details. No requests submitted. This file is derived analysis and a source inventory, not original-file custody.
