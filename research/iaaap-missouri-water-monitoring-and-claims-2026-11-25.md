# IAAAP — Missouri water monitoring and public claims guidance
2026-11-25. Supplemental public accountability record; not an all-clear or legal advice.

- PROVEN: Missouri announced a multi-agency response on November 20 and activated its State Emergency Operations Center on November 24. Missouri DNR reports coordination with ten public drinking-water systems using surface water and identifies thirteen intakes in the stated monitoring area. These are system/intake counts, not counts of contaminated water supplies. No validated post-event results located in the cited state page.
- PROVEN: Missouri's November 24 statement compares modeled public exposure to the OSHA occupational lead limit. OSHA's limit is 50 micrograms per cubic meter averaged over eight hours for covered workers; EPA's ambient lead standard is 0.15 micrograms per cubic meter over a three-month average. Different populations and averaging periods make these non-interchangeable; neither establishes a universal short-term community safety threshold. SUSPECTED: public communication could benefit from explicit population and averaging-period clarification. No measured exceedance or violation established.
- PROVEN: Missouri DNR's claims FAQ describes a two-year period from the incident date. Both 28 USC 2401(b) and 10 USC 2733(b)(1) instead use accrual as the ordinary statutory trigger; FTCA also specifies a six-month period following mailing of final denial. Accrual can coincide with the incident, but need not in every case. Eligibility and individual deadlines depend on facts and applicable law; prompt professional advice is appropriate. No compensation or liability established.
- Missouri DNR acknowledges that pre-event soil concentrations may be unavailable in some cases. This is a reason to preserve existing background data, not proof of contamination or missing mandatory samples.
- November 25: Reuters reports that the operation occurred and attributes confirmation to FEMA. Its displayed update timestamp is inconsistent with its narrative chronology, so exact timing is not treated as verified; the direct agency confirmation was not independently retrieved. No final treatment-completion certification, all-clear, or validated environmental results established here.
