## State records and conditional deadlines
Permit pp. 275–278, visually inspected: G4 annual compliance certification, G5 semiannual monitoring report, and G6 emissions inventory are due March 31, 2027 for relevant 2026 reporting periods; G6 provides July 1 annual fee timing. G10 requires retention of applicable compliance monitoring records and supporting data for at least five years. G13 (state-only) requires reporting qualifying actual, imminent or probable atmospheric hazardous releases to Iowa DNR and local police or sheriff within six hours of discovery/onset, followed by a written report under 567 IAC 131.2(2). The Iowa legislature's published chapter specifies 30 days for written reporting and subsequent findings as available. A qualifying November 25 trigger would yield a nominal December 25 date, subject to applicable timing rules and actual trigger; an earlier March trigger is possible but unverified. Reporting applicability, receipt, exact trigger date and any exceptions are not established. Nondetection alone does not resolve an imminent/probable release criterion. No violation is alleged.
