# IAAAP — administrative record and permittee reconciliation, 2026-11-25

Public accountability research, not a safety advisory. Existing IAAAP procurement, permit, monitoring and Missouri notes reviewed. No post-treatment results verified.

## New primary-source findings
- EPA's November 3 public notice for the third storage emergency permit (effective September 29 through December 26, 2026) identifies a 45-day administrative-record review at Burlington Public Library and EPA Region 7 Records Center. November 3 plus 45 elapsed days is December 18; this is a provisional review-window estimate, not a confirmed legal deadline. Obtain the actual opening and closing dates, index and copies before access arrangements change.
- EPA's November 16 public notice for the separate treatment/storage permit (effective November 13, 2026) says another notice will announce availability of its administrative record. No such later notice was located in this limited search. The eight response-site documents are not necessarily the complete administrative record. Request both indices, version histories, redaction authorities and the date of public availability.
- EPA's archived April 20, 2020 notice names both the Army and American Ordnance LLC as emergency permittees for a different hazardous-waste incident. Both 2026 notices name only the Army. This is a documented change in named permittees, NOT proof that the contractor was released from liability or that the incidents share a cause. Obtain the relevant responsibility clauses, permit applications, operator designation and correspondence explaining the difference.
- GAO-11-488R (2011) describes the October 2008 American Ordnance operation/maintenance contract: no direct government cost for specified services in exchange for authorized use of government facilities and equipment. The arrangement is not proof that production, remediation, or today's response costs are zero or that the original terms still govern. Obtain current executed contract/modifications, environmental duties, indemnity provisions and cost allocations. The 2024 EPA joint penalty resolution confirms a separate enforcement history, not a finding of responsibility for this incident.
