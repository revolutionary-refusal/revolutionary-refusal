# IAAAP — Iowa reporting rule transition and cost recovery
As of 2026-11-26. Public environmental and financial accountability only.

## New evidence
- Iowa ARC 0486D was adopted July 21, 2026, filed July 24, published August 19, and effective September 23. It replaced chapters 131, 133 and 137 with chapter 105. The DNR spill guidance still lists chapters 131 and 133. The legislature's rule 131.2 PDF visual rendering explicitly confirms rescission, despite stale extracted text. The March discovery and November response therefore fall on different sides of a rule transition. Do not apply the old chapter to a new November event.
- New rule 105.5 retains the six-hour initial notification deadline for qualifying hazardous conditions, clarifies that the 30-day follow-up runs from the initial report, and requires later findings/lab results to be sent to DNR as available. For a qualifying initial report on November 25, 30 elapsed days gives December 25, 2026; illustrative only, not an established filing deadline. The prior rule required a written report within 30 days and used “should” for subsequent findings; the new rule uses mandatory language. Iowa Code 455B.381 includes probable or imminent releases; 455B.390 limits jurisdiction for solely internal conditions and certain regulated activities. Whether the March discovery or November event triggered these rules, and any federal immunity, exemption or waiver, remain unverified.
- Iowa Code 455B.392 provides a conditional cost recovery route for reasonable state/local cleanup and evacuation costs, natural-resource damages, and extraordinary response costs. Facility liability under subsection 1 is capped at $50 million under subsection 2; this is a statutory ceiling, not a claim, debt, estimate, or proof of liability. Section 455B.396 provides a 120-day period to perfect a qualifying lien from incurrence of costs; November 25 +120 days = March 25, 2027, only as an illustration. No lien against the federal installation is presumed. Section 455B.397 provides for confidential financial reports only when liability exists; request public aggregate claims and receipts, not confidential submissions.
- NRC and state/local notification files are separate corroboration channels. CERCLA section 103 and EPCRA section 304 have conditional reporting triggers and federally permitted release exceptions. The Coast Guard warns that public NRC reports are initial and unvalidated. Its 2026 workbook was linked but not retrieved. EPA site 17211 still listed eight documents; no new validated post-event laboratory report was retrieved. Iowa DNR's Field Office Compliance search interface was reached but incident-specific records were not retrieved. Favorable EPA initial airborne nondetections remain counterevidence to claims of substantial airborne release.
