# Government Debt & Recovery Tracker

Started: 2026-11-22
Scope: Publicly documented U.S. government-related fraud restitution, recoveries, debts and unresolved financial obligations. This is an evidence ledger, not a claim that accounting adjustments are debts.

## Rules
- Record debtor, creditor, legal basis, original amount, due date, amount paid, outstanding balance, as-of date, source, status.
- Never label fraud proceeds, seized assets, settlement amounts or unsupported journal entries as outstanding debt without a documented obligation.
- Do not sum overlapping amounts or treat forfeiture and restitution as interchangeable.
- Unknown balances stay UNKNOWN until supported by records.
