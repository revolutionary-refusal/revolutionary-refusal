# Government Debt & Recovery Tracker

Started: 2026-11-22
Scope: Publicly documented U.S. government-related fraud restitution, recoveries, debts and unresolved financial obligations. This is an evidence ledger, not a claim that accounting adjustments are debts.

## Rules
- Record debtor, creditor, legal basis, original amount, due date, amount paid, outstanding balance, as-of date, source, status.
- Never label fraud proceeds, seized assets, settlement amounts or unsupported journal entries as outstanding debt without a documented obligation.
- Do not sum overlapping amounts or treat forfeiture and restitution as interchangeable.
- Unknown balances stay UNKNOWN until supported by records.

### Historical national baseline (not current balance)

GAO-18-203, issued 2018-02-02: at FY2016 close, $110 billion federal criminal restitution outstanding, including $100 billion then identified by U.S. Attorney's Offices as uncollectible because offenders lacked ability to pay. GAO reports $2.95 billion collected in FY2014–2016 and $33.9 billion ordered for offenders sentenced in that period. These have different time/population scopes; no recovery percentage or net balance should be calculated by dividing/subtracting them. The $100 billion is a subset, not an additional debt. No current national figure established in this review.
- https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-18-203
