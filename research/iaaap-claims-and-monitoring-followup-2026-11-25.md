SF95 instructions (reverse, inspected page image) require a specific monetary amount and receipt by the appropriate agency rather than mailing alone. 28 USC 2401(b) generally sets two years from accrual for administrative presentation and six months after mailed final denial for litigation; 2675(a) also provides an option to deem six months without final disposition a denial. These are general FTCA provisions, not a determination of eligibility, individual accrual or payment. Sampling requests and FEMA assistance should not be assumed to constitute tort claim presentation.
