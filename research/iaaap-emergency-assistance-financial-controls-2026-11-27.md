FEMA's incident page returned HTTP 403; incident-specific federal cost share and covered programs remain unverified. The Army's 24 November release describes FEMA-supported temporary accommodation through 27 November with extensions possible. This is not a verified termination of benefits.

42 USC 5193, official compilation through 10 November 2026: eligible Title V emergency assistance has a federal share of at least 75%; the ordinary total assistance limit per emergency is $5 million. The President can exceed it on specified findings and must report to Congress when exceeded. This limit is not a cap on all Army/EPA incident expenditures and is not an award, payment, or evidence of a breach. Section 5192 permits federal agency assistance with or without reimbursement. Section 5155 prohibits duplicative benefits for the same loss, but permits assistance for uncovered portions and conditional assistance before other benefits arrive. No duplicative payment, debtor or intentional wrongdoing is established.

Accounting custody: FEMA mission assignments and reimbursement records; Army and EPA cost ledgers; state emergency-management expenditure records; Treasury disbursements. Net interagency reimbursements against the underlying expenditure when consolidating; distinguish commitments, obligations, disbursements and reimbursements.
