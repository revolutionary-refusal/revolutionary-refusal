# IAAAP — water quality exchange and submission receipts
27 November 2026

EPA's Water Quality Exchange (WQX) FAQ identifies a structured public water-data publication route and processing event logs that record submission warnings and errors. Its published refresh schedule is Wednesday and Friday around 02:00 Eastern. This schedule concerns data already submitted to WQX; it is not an incident reporting deadline. USGS independently documents the collaborative Water Quality Portal and a separate USGS Samples API for USGS-origin observations. A duplicate portal record is not an independent sample. EPA's November 26 incident update and eight-document site 17211 register were rechecked; no newly validated incident measurements retrieved. No WQX submission or incident-specific USGS sample established.

PROVEN: documented data exchange mechanism and source-specific API; published incident update. SUSPECTED: exclusive reliance on a live incident dashboard may limit reproducibility. UNVERIFIED: whether incident samples are eligible for, submitted to, or published through WQX; laboratory validation, independent samples, and actual expenditure.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7 and state water agencies separately: decision whether to publish eligible incident water samples in WQX; submission dates, organization/activity/result identifiers, aggregate processing receipts and warnings, correction history, and validated results with qualifiers and detection limits. USGS separately: whether independent relevant observations exist. Request only releasable records, excluding private addresses and sensitive details. Preserve original lab files apart from derived exchange records. No request submitted.
