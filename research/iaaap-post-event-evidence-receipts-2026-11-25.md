# IAAAP post-event evidence receipts — 2026-11-25

## Findings and counterevidence
- Iowa Department of Agriculture's November 25 post-event guidance anticipates no adverse livestock or food safety impacts based on available information, and directs producers seeking additional assurance to consult veterinarians about herd testing. This is a new post-event statement compared with November 24 guidance; it expressly relies on EPA monitoring. It is not an independent laboratory result or proof of no agricultural exposure.
- EPA's November 25 statement reports nondetection by its real-time monitors in the first hour. The Washington Post separately reports a positive on-site reading attributed to an unnamed Army official. The underlying Army measurement, units, sampling method, instrument validation and location category (onsite versus community) are not authenticated. This is a reconciliation lead, not proof of an EPA error; different monitoring populations and methods could both yield valid results. Do not compare the reported concentration with regulatory limits without verified units and averaging period.
- Emergency permit p.17, V.A (page image inspected) requires same-day written notifications to EPA for formal decisions on the order to proceed, order to execute and actual treatment date/time. Receipt records are distinct from press releases. The November 25 state and federal announcements support completion but do not authenticate the required submissions. EPA response site 17211 still lists the dashboard; no receipt or final validated data located in reviewed sources. Nonpublication is not proof of non-submission.
- Permit p.16, IV.U.2.ii requires initiation of specified soil and surface sampling within 24 hours, subject to the modeled deposition criterion and access permission for private property. The requirement is initiation, not completion or publication of all results.
