# IAAAP — Illinois child care cost accountability (2026-11-25)

## PROVEN: additional financial mechanism
Illinois Department of Early Childhood (IDEC) November 24, 2026 incident advisory expressly reminds licensed centers of the Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP) attendance exemption for extraordinary disruptions. It also describes the ordinary 70% attendance threshold for payment based on eligible days. The linked administrative policy 02.06.01, last revised May 1, 2022, defines qualifying low attendance as at least 50% of approved children absent, or closure due to an extraordinary event. Approval is case-specific; documentation and a signed decision are required. The policy's ten-day notification language is ambiguous; obtain current instructions rather than assert a filing deadline. IDEC became the lead CCAP agency July 1, 2026, so the legacy IDHS policy contact is not evidence of current processing responsibility.

Illinois reported partial state emergency operations activation on November 24 and continued standby pending federal final results on November 25. No additional validated post-event measurements were established in this review. No financial loss or exposure is inferred from eligibility guidance. Illinois' August 7, 2026 CCAP payment update identifies the Comptroller vendor payment portal as a separate payment verification route; it does not establish incident-related disbursements.
