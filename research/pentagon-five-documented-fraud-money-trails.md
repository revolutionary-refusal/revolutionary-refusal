# Pentagon Financial Misconduct — Five Documented Money Trails

Date compiled: 2026-11-22

## Scope and caution
Five distinct Defense Department-related fraud/theft investigations with reported payment routes. These are **not** demonstrated components of the FY1999 $2.3 trillion unsupported accounting adjustments. Criminal pleas, convictions, civil settlements, and allegations are distinguished below. Prior research cross-checked some figures with multiple sources, but **four independent checks for every case have not been established**. Treat amounts as source-reported pending review of the underlying court records.

## 1. Air Force IT contracts — Alan Hayward James
- Reported scheme: more than $37 million in inflated military IT contracts; funds diverted to conspirators and bribes.
- Disposition: guilty plea reported April 2026; minimum $1.45 million restitution commitment reported.
- Flow: Air Force -> inflated IT contracts -> conspirators/bribes.
- Sources:
  - https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-member-air-force-pleads-guilty-multi-year-bid-rigging-schemes-and-conspiracy-defraud
  - https://www.justice.gov/atr/media/1434041/dl

## 2. Navy contracts — Cory Taylor Wright
- Reported scheme: false contract documents and invoices, with insider benefits and kickbacks.
- Disposition: guilty plea and 2026 sentence; $9,128,515 restitution reported.
- Flow: Navy -> prime contractor / CCP -> fraudulent billings and insider benefits.
- Source:
  - https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/veteran-sentenced-3-years-federal-prison-defrauding-navy-out-more-9-million-bid

## 3. Army technology contracts — Carlos Duque
- Reported scheme: bribery involving contractors Kent and Pick; Army reportedly paid their company more than $3 million.
- Disposition: Duque pleaded guilty October 2026; $55,166.70 reported bribe/forfeiture amount. Other defendants' charges remain allegations unless separately adjudicated.
- Flow: Army -> prime contractor -> subcontract -> company -> Duque.
- Sources:
  - https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-army-official-pleads-guilty-conspiracy-bribery-and-major-fraud-related-hawaii-defense
  - https://www.justice.gov/atr/media/1470216/dl

## 4. Raytheon / RTX — procurement pricing and billing
- Reported misconduct: defective pricing information and double-billing allegations in government contracting.
- Disposition: 2024 civil settlement and separate criminal resolution; $428 million is a settlement figure, **not** an adjudicated theft amount.
- Flow: Pentagon contracts -> disputed inflated prices / duplicate charges -> Raytheon.
- Sources:
  - https://www.justice.gov/d9/2024-10/settlement_agreement_1.pdf
  - https://www.justice.gov/usao-ma/pr/raytheon-agrees-pay-over-950-million-connection-defective-pricing-foreign-bribery-and

## 5. Army contractor phantom employment — Thomas Bouchard and Chantelle Boyd
- Reported scheme: largely fictitious employment and government-funded personal travel.
- Disposition: guilty pleas and sentences; $487,658.87 restitution order against Bouchard reported.
- Flow: Army -> contractor Evolution Enterprise -> Boyd's employment and travel expenses.
- Source:
  - https://www.justice.gov/usao-ma/pr/maryland-woman-sentenced-conspiring-defraud-government

## Aggregate context
- GAO reports approximately $10.8 billion in confirmed Defense Department fraud during 2017–2024; **do not add this figure to the five cases or imply it is the total for them**.
