# IAAAP — environmental liability accounting (26 November 2026)

PROVEN: DoD Financial Management Regulation 7000.14-R, volume 4 chapter 13 (June 2024), sections 3.2.1–2, 3.3.4, 3.4.7 and 4.2, establishes recognition criteria for probable, reasonably estimable environmental liabilities, annual estimate review, transaction-level expenditure reconciliation, retention of supporting documentation for the life of the liability, and documented September 30 roll-forward assessments. Section 3.4.3 provides contingent-liability disclosure criteria. A March 2026 hazard discovery predates the FY2026 cutoff; November 25 response operations fall in FY2027. Neither date alone determines recognition; the relevant accounting criteria and evidence must be tested. The FY2026 assessment, amount, classification, appropriation, payments, contractor responsibility and subsequent adjustments are UNVERIFIED.

Army FY2025 Annual Financial Report General Fund Note 14 (pp. 113–114) reports $43,800,926,000 in environmental and disposal liabilities based on Q3 information because year-end totals were unavailable during the shutdown. The 13 components reconcile exactly to that figure. Note 11 reports $41,228,978,000 of that category not covered by budgetary resources (94.13%; remainder $2,571,948,000). These are Army-wide reported estimates, not IAAAP costs, theft, cash payments or delinquent debt. The FY2025 independent auditor's Exhibit I H, pp. 70–71, identifies weaknesses including timely recognition of newly identified event-driven sites. OIG announced FY2026 General Fund audit oversight on December 1, 2025 (project D2026-D000FI-0010.000). FY2026 final report not retrieved in this review. No IAAAP-specific omission established.
