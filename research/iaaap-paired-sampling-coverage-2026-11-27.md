# IAAAP — paired sampling coverage checkpoint
27 November 2026

EPA's 26 November primary release reports repeat sampling at more than 130 structures. CBS News, updated 27 November at 18:08 EST, attributes to EPA a new 88% completion figure for repeat sampling at homes that had baseline samples. This is a secondary report of agency progress; the underlying 27 November agency statement and exact denominator were not located. The 12% arithmetic complement applies only to that home cohort, not all homes or all samples. CBS's separate figure of more than 1,600 baseline samples is not independently reconciled with EPA's published 1,591 count on 26 November. The figures may reflect different intake cutoffs. No new validated concentrations retrieved; incident register lists eight documents.

EPA's original 25 November announcement also exists on the GovDelivery distribution service, timestamped 09:23 EST. This provides a second distribution location for the 1,216-sample announcement, not independent field measurements or an archival copy held by this investigator.

PROVEN: primary 26 November repeat-structure count, timestamped distribution copy, eight-document register. UNVERIFIED: primary source for 88%, denominator, later baseline count, validated paired measurements. SUSPECTED: sampling coverage cannot be assessed fully without cohort denominators. Counterevidence: EPA reports favorable real-time air monitoring, which cannot establish soil or water outcomes. No evidence of improper sampling or concealed results.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7 and participating state agencies separately: dated baseline/follow-up counts by matrix and property class, denominators and exclusions, deidentified paired sample IDs, sampling and validation dates, duplicates and rejected samples, and publication revision history.
