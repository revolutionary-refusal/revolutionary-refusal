# IAAAP — notification integrity, permit responsibility and legacy baseline
2026-11-25, pre-event review (approximately 12:09 UTC). Accountability research, not a safety advisory.

## New verified-in-source findings
1. Iowa HHS lead-surveillance page section marked updated November 24 at 4:24 p.m. contains the inconsistent date “Wednesday, November 24.” November 24, 2026 was Tuesday; the Army's November 24 release specifies Wednesday November 25. This is a public-notice accuracy issue, not evidence of intent or a changed schedule. Preserve the original revision, correction timestamp and distribution logs; refer to current official guidance.
2. EPA's third storage permit was issued September 29 and its public web notice is dated November 3 (35 elapsed calendar days). The treatment permit issued November 13 has a web notice dated November 16 (3 days). The historical March 2, 2020 permit has a web notice dated April 20 (49 days). 40 CFR 270.61(b)(5) requires accompanying public notice; these web publication dates do not establish the first notice by any method or a legal violation. Seek dated notice publication and distribution evidence and the agency's interpretation.
3. EPA's 2020 notice names the Army AND American Ordnance as permittees; the November 2026 permit names the Army as owner and operator. EPA's separate Superfund profile attributes day-to-day operation to American Ordnance; its company website identifies it as part of Day & Zimmermann and describes storage and environmental duties. This establishes roles, not incident-specific contractual liability. The 2020 permit concerned different waste; the two permit scopes are not interchangeable. November 2026 permit pp. 3–4 requires corrective action as necessary inside and outside the installation and expressly preserves enforcement pathways. The full contract and insurance/indemnity terms remain unverified.
4. EPA Superfund site 0700413 is a separate ongoing historical cleanup record. Current schedule lists NPL finalization August 30, 1990, latest five-year review March 10, 2026, construction completion and delisting not yet achieved. An older indexed version gave March 11 for the 2026 review; signed original needed to resolve date. EPA lists lead as a historical soil contaminant in OU1 and OU4, with explicit caveat that listed contaminants may already have been remediated. Installation restoration administrative record advertises October 2026 index and quarterly 2026 RAB minutes identifiers 8131–8134; warns some online files omit laboratory packages. Fifth-review heading visible but report not retrieved. Historical contamination cannot be attributed to the November incident; matched pre/post sampling and source attribution are needed.
