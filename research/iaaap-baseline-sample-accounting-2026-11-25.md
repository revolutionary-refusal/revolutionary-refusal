# IAAAP — baseline sampling accounting and initial results
2026-11-25 | Public environmental accountability; not an all-clear.

## Findings
- IAI-2026-11-25-BS1 — EPA's November 25 release reports 1,216 pre-event samples: water 210, soil 650, dust 297, air 59. Sum agrees. Soil represents 53.45% and air 4.85% of the count. These are sample counts, not unique locations, validated results or coverage percentages. Yesterday's statement of more than 1,000 samples is compatible; exact daily increment cannot be inferred from a lower bound.
- BS2 — The same EPA release reports 300 agency staff and contractors deployed, and a five-minute air-monitoring publication interval during the event. It reports no lead detected in pre-event air sampling. Personnel count is not labor-hours or cost; published frequency is not demonstrated completeness or analytical validation.
- BS3 — November 25 news reports attribute preliminary post-event air nondetections to EPA monitoring and an interview with the EPA administrator. This is encouraging counterevidence to predictions of substantial airborne exposure, not proof of zero exposure or a final all-clear. Dashboard records were not independently retrieved; detection limits, coverage, soil/water results and validated laboratory reports remain unverified.
- BS4 — Iowa governor's November 24 statement records a request to the Army for additional information needed for state response planning and an assurance that it would be supplied. Delivery, contents and timing are unverified; the statement does not establish withholding or a missed obligation. The Army's November 25 statement separately promises a status update by 11 a.m. CST; the initial statement is not a completion certificate.

## Unsent oversight scopes

Request preserved dashboard exports and separate state/laboratory copies.
EPA finance/procurement and Iowa emergency management: request incident-level obligations, expenditures, contractor task orders, aggregate staff hours and reimbursement accounting, distinguishing commitments from payment.
Army and Iowa governor's office: request the November 24 information request, transmittal receipts and public or segregable response documents; Army/EPA: completion certification and post-event environmental reports. No submissions made.
