# IAAAP — permit duration and public notice reconciliation (26 November 2026)

PROVEN — EPA's signed 13 November emergency permit, page 1, specifies effectiveness at 00:00 CST on 13 November 2026 and expiration at 23:59 CST on 11 February 2027. Page 19 displays signature on 13 November at 13:04:57 CST. Page 2 states that this permit supersedes the 29 September storage permit, previously scheduled to expire 26 December. The two expiry dates are not two concurrent authorizations.

The date difference is 90 days, but the printed timestamps span 90 days 23 hours 59 minutes (91 calendar dates inclusive). 40 CFR 270.61(b)(2) caps emergency permits at 90 days. SUSPECTED — a counting convention or drafting discrepancy merits clarification. The signature timestamp is distinct from the printed effective time; counting calendar days excluding the initial day gives 90 days. No legal interpretation or adjudication establishing a violation was located. No conclusion about validity, unlawful activity, or environmental effects follows from the arithmetic. The 25 November event occurred well before either interpretation of the expiry.

EPA's third storage permit webpage is dated 3 November, 35 elapsed days after its 29 September issuance. The replacement permit notice is dated 16 November, three days after issuance. The regulation requires accompanying notice; these website dates do not establish first publication across all channels. Prior newspaper notices, mailings, administrative record and any amendments remain unverified.
