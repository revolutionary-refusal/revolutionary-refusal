# IAAAP — monitoring data access and emergency expenditure accountability
2026-11-25. Pre-event public accountability record, not a safety advisory.

- PROVEN (Illinois EPA, November 24): Illinois reported that federal EPA was developing a public monitoring dashboard and that Illinois officials were still seeking details about federal air monitoring. The state's emergency operations center was partially activated. On November 25 its page was revised at 7:13 a.m. CT, 60 minutes after the previously preserved revision; the forecast of no Illinois plume impact remained. No validated post-event results were located. A planned dashboard is not an operational dataset; federal and state repetition of the same forecast is not independent measurement.
- PROVEN (Iowa emergency management, November 24): Iowa announced up to 100 federally activated National Guard personnel and outreach to approximately 150 households, in addition to the state emergency proclamation. These are authorized/planned resource figures, not actual attendance, expenditure, or number assisted. Iowa DNR reported continued assessment of federal monitoring plans. The state explicitly distinguishes the Army's operational responsibility from the Guard's support role. Obtain aggregate actual personnel-days, obligations, expenditures and reimbursement allocation by agency, without personal or operational details.
- PROVEN (EPA site 17211, checked November 25): the register still lists eight documents; the public profile says redactions were requested by the Army for critical infrastructure security. Illinois' request for monitoring information is not proof of unlawful withholding. Request public or segregable aggregate data, version histories, lab validation, and record identifiers, not protected technical details.
- SUSPECTED: an evidence handoff gap between federal monitoring and independent state assessment. UNVERIFIED: dashboard launch, actual exposure, actual assistance and expenditures, procurement cancellation payments, contractor liability. No new signed procurement or financial responsibility instrument located.
