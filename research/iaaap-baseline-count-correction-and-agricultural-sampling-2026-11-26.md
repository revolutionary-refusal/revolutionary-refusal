# IAAAP — baseline count correction and agricultural sampling — 2026-11-26

## Append-only correction
The previous note compared EPA's November 25 rounded statement of 1,200 baseline samples to the November 26 intake total of 1,591 (difference 391). EPA's separate November 25 pre-event release actually itemizes 210 water, 650 soil, 297 dust and 59 air samples, totaling 1,216. Against that detailed baseline, the November 26 total is 375 higher (30.84%). The earlier comparison remains arithmetically valid for the rounded statement, but is not the best count reconciliation. The difference of 16 between the two November 25 statements is consistent with rounding; neither difference proves missing samples or newly collected samples. Revised medium-specific counts and accession history are not yet available in the sources inspected.

## Agricultural accountability
EPA's November 26 update explicitly reports pre/post sampling of nearby farms in partnership with USDA, and further sampling of agricultural ponds in Missouri. This advances the earlier unresolved question of whether farms received paired sampling, but does not establish the number of farms, media, matching sample IDs, completeness, or validated concentrations. Iowa agriculture officials on November 25 expressed a favorable preliminary assessment based on EPA monitoring. Missouri DNR's post-event update likewise attributes its air findings to EPA; these are not independent laboratory confirmations. USDA Rural Development's November 25 notice confirms coordination with Iowa agriculture officials. EPA response register 17211 still lists eight documents; no new validated laboratory results were retrieved in this review.

## Records scope (unsent)
EPA Region 7 and USDA separately: aggregate farm sample counts by medium and state, paired pre/post identifiers without private locations, collection versus accession timestamps, laboratory assignment, quality flags/reporting limits, and reconciliation of the 1,216 and 1,591 totals. Iowa agriculture and Missouri DNR: records of data versions received, independently collected samples and aggregate test results, and non-sensitive expenditure summaries. No private addresses or personal data requested.

PROVEN: dated agency statements and arithmetic. SUSPECTED: rounding explains the 16-sample difference; intake reconciliation may explain the later change. UNVERIFIED: complete sampling coverage, independently validated results and actual expenditures. No new environmental outcome asserted. No external requests submitted.
