# IAAAP — analytical scope and sampling denominator clarification
28 November 2026 (UTC observation).

This supplements the existing sampling-count and initial-water-timing notes without replacing either. EPA's November 28 release describes a broader analytical program than lead alone: selected volatile organic compounds in air and selected semivolatile compounds in soil, but total lead only in water and dust wipes. It identifies soil method 8270 and air method TO-15. EPA's separate method descriptions corroborate the analytical categories, not actual incident implementation or measured concentrations. This is a statement of analytical scope, not a finding of contamination. The initial public-water nondetection cannot establish nondetection of other analytes or in other media.

EPA's release explicitly identifies the reported 88% as completion of follow-up sampling among homes sampled before the event, not the proportion of results that were clean or unchanged. This corroborates the earlier secondary-source attribution of the denominator type. Exact home counts, valid paired results and field completion times are not independently verified. Its aggregate statistics retain the previously identified future-dated cutoff discrepancy; no aggregate counts are released from the existing verification hold. EPA separately describes cessation of the Xact real-time monitoring modality on November 26, not cessation of all environmental air sampling. Incident register still lists eight records.
