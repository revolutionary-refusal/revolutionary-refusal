# IAAAP — Historical notice and waste classification
2026-11-26 | Public administrative and environmental accountability.

An Army public-affairs article dated March 21, 2014 attributes the beginning of a research project in 2004 to Defense Department concerns about deteriorating lead azide at IAAAP. This is a contemporaneously published (2014) retrospective account of a 2004 concern, not a 2004 original inspection report. It predates the September 2025 neutralization notice and the March 11, 2026 inspection. The calendar-year difference between 2004 and 2026 is 22 years; the 2014 article predates the March 11 inspection by 11 years, 11 months and 18 days. No evidence reviewed identifies the older stockpile as the same inventory involved in the 2026 response. Different inventories, intervening disposal or changes in condition are plausible. General historical awareness does not prove knowledge of the specific 2026 hazard or contractor fault.

The Army's July 10, 2026 storage-permit extension announcement says the material had been reclassified as hazardous waste because it was no longer needed for production. This provides an explicit public upper bound for the reported classification decision, not the actual signed decision date. EPA independently confirms the waste classification in its November 16 notice. The September 2025 SAM notice described an anticipated procurement; it did not establish that classification had already occurred. The actual order, signed classification decision, original inspection history, MuniRem modifications and payments remain unverified.

EPA site 17211 lists eight documents on November 26. No newly validated post-event laboratory data was retrieved in this review. The November 25 preliminary airborne lead nondetection is favorable evidence and not a substitute for validated results for other media.

Unsent records scope: Army historical records custodian and contracting office: releasable records of the 2004 concern, disposition or transfer of the historical stockpile, non-operational administrative identifiers establishing whether inventory populations overlap, dated waste classification determination and signoff, contract responsibility and notifications; EPA Region 7 independently: receipt dates, administrative classification documentation and validated aggregate environmental results. Request no facility security details, technical procedures or private information.

PROVEN: dated public statements and regulatory notice. SUSPECTED: historical concern could relate to later disposal planning, but linkage is not established. UNVERIFIED: inventory identity, exact classification date, contractor liability, actual payments, environmental outcomes. No inference of wrongdoing from timing. Sources are linked, not original archival copies.
