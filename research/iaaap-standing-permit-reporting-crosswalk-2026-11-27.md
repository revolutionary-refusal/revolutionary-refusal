# IAAAP — standing permit reporting crosswalk
27 November 2026 | Derived research note, not an original-document archive.

EPA's September 2018 final hazardous-waste permit (IA7213820445) names the Army as owner and American Ordnance LLC as operator. EPA's current permit index separately identifies this September 2018 document. The 13 November 2026 emergency permit names the Army alone and concerns a distinct authorized activity. It does not establish release of the operator from obligations elsewhere.

Standing permit printed page 95, VIII.L and VIII.M: underlying monitoring and quality-control records belong in the operating record; written groundwater reports for May annual sampling are due September 30. The next printed page contains the transition to corrective action. Printed page 114, IX.Q: for units or areas with a selected final RCRA remedy, corrective-measure implementation reports on the prior year's performance are due March 1; these include sampling analysis, remedy effectiveness and deficiencies. IX.R establishes a five-year evaluation cycle from remedy selection. These provide separate historical baseline and performance records, not automatic requirements for every November 2026 emergency sample. Verify amendments, applicability and actual filing receipts before drawing compliance conclusions.

Nominal records checkpoints: September 30, 2026 for the May 2026 annual groundwater event; March 1, 2027 for qualifying 2026 corrective measures. Earlier Army reporting dated September 2, 2026 describes progress on RDX groundwater cleanup; it is not evidence of November lead results or submission of the required annual report. EPA emergency response register still shows eight documents on 27 November; latest retrieved EPA release is November 26. No new validated post-event measurements retrieved. Zero detection in preliminary air monitoring is not a conclusion about all environmental media.

PROVEN: published permit terms, EPA index and agency statements. SUSPECTED: historical baseline and incident data may be difficult to reconcile without a common record index. UNVERIFIED: current applicability, filing receipts, amendments, laboratory results and actual expenditures.
