# IAAAP — media denominator and water benchmark cross-check
28 November 2026 (UTC observation).

Reuters' article dated 28 November, displayed update 09:55 UTC, attributes unchanged soil and water lead concentrations to laboratory results from 88% of post-event samples. EPA's contemporaneously retrieved release instead describes 88% as repeat collection coverage among previously sampled homes; it says tap, soil, dust and air laboratory data are still being prepared for validation/release. The article may rely on another statement or dashboard data, but the underlying results and matching denominator were not retrievable: EPA's dashboard redirect returned no readable records. The Reuters percentage and headline therefore do not independently verify unchanged soil concentrations. Do not amplify the claim as a validated result. This is a new media cross-check, not a replacement for the existing sampling-scope note.

EPA reports four baseline untreated-water detections among 35 source locations, all outside its reported actual plume area; 4/35 = 11.43% of these source locations, not a population estimate. EPA's 15 ppb comparison is numerically true for the reported readings, but the Lead and Copper Rule action level concerns a water system's tap sampling distribution and triggers treatment actions; it is not a universal safe concentration or a raw-source compliance threshold. EPA lists the health goal as zero, and the revised 10 ppb action level begins 1 November 2027. Mixed raw and finished water follow-up cannot establish matched changes without sample-level records. The existing release cutoff hold remains in place.

PROVEN: published texts and difference in denominator descriptions. SUSPECTED: media conflation of collection coverage with result coverage; a distinct underlying dataset remains possible. UNVERIFIED: complete validated soil results, source of Reuters' percentage, matched water samples, exact reporting limits and release version history. Eight documents listed in EPA incident register. No new lab-level results retrieved.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7, state drinking-water agencies, participating utilities and laboratories separately: dated, deidentified source/finished/tap and paired soil data, raw-to-validated result counts, exact denominators and qualifiers, dataset version and publication history; retain independently held laboratory records. No request sent. Derived note only, not original-source archive.
