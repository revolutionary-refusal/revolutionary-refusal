# IAAAP agricultural sampling and claims guidance — 2026-11-25

## Verified changes
Iowa Department of Agriculture and Land Stewardship (IDALS) issued livestock guidance November 18, 2026 (date confirmed by its news index) and revised it November 24. The revision explicitly advises producers to record relevant expenses both before and after the operation for potential Army claims. The earlier page already provided a livestock/property damage claims route, but lacked these two expense-recording instructions. The revision also specifies a 20-mile advisory scope and retains advice to request Army baseline and follow-up soil and forage testing, with self-arranged sampling if Army service is unavailable. These are recommendations, not mandatory claims criteria or proof of reimbursement entitlement.

EPA's November 25 baseline release reports 1,216 samples across four broad categories, without a separate forage or livestock count. The published tally therefore cannot verify coverage of farms or paired baseline/post-event samples. This does not demonstrate that forage was not sampled. EPA's initial airborne nondetection is favorable but does not establish agricultural outcomes. No new validated post-treatment laboratory dataset was located in the sources reviewed.

## Records requests (not sent)

## QA and custody
The state revision does not establish a change in law, an admission of liability, contamination or actual expenses. State guidance and EPA sampling totals have distinct purposes. EPA's dashboard is a mutable publication interface; request versioned exports and independent state laboratory records. This note is a derived source inventory, not original-source custody.
