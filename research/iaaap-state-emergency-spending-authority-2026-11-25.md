# IAAAP — Iowa emergency spending authority, November 25, 2026

## New primary record
The signed Iowa disaster proclamation dated November 24, 2026 (both pages visually inspected, governor signature and secretary of state attestation on page 2) establishes a state-level spending-control exception. Sections 2–4 allow qualifying state employee overtime retroactive to the initial emergency operations center activation, temporarily suspend specified competitive-selection provisions for necessary emergency purchases by responding state agencies, and set December 24, 2026 as the expiration unless terminated earlier or extended in writing. The November 20 activation announcement provides a public starting checkpoint for payroll reconciliation, subject to actual activation records. The covered counties are Des Moines, Lee and Van Buren. This extends earlier research that documented the proclamation but not its procurement and payroll provisions.

PROVEN: signed instrument and published activation announcement. The state exception is restricted by purpose and duration; it is not blanket authorization for unrelated spending, nor proof that any particular purchase or overtime payment occurred. The signed text does not expressly activate the individual assistance grant program; other state proclamations do so explicitly. This does not establish ineligibility or absence of other assistance. A state declaration is not a presidential Stafford Act declaration. Federal local-vendor preference under FAR 26.202 depends on a qualifying presidential declaration and covered emergency contracts; applicability here remains UNVERIFIED. FEMA assistance alone does not establish that trigger. No verified new environmental laboratory results or contractor payment figures in this check.
