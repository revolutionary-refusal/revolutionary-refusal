# IAAAP — waste classification and retention checkpoint (26 November 2026)

PROVEN (regulatory text, not a finding of noncompliance): 40 CFR 266.202(b) establishes four alternative circumstances in which unused military munitions become solid waste, including deterioration beyond reasonable restoration or reuse, or a declaration by an authorized military official. The classification date is not necessarily the date of physical disposal or the date of an emergency permit. 40 CFR 262.11(a), (f) separately requires a hazardous-waste determination when waste is generated and reassessment when relevant properties may change; applicable small and large quantity generators must retain supporting records for at least three years after the last qualifying transfer to treatment, storage or disposal. Unresolved enforcement or an Administrator request extends retention. If 25 November 2026 is the last qualifying transfer, the illustrative minimum endpoint is 25 November 2029; later cleanup or other transfers may change the relevant date. No actual destruction date for records is established.

EPA's November 16 notice confirms the November 13 emergency permit covers hazardous waste. The September 2025 procurement and March 2026 discovery dates in earlier research cannot by themselves establish when the material legally became waste. The governing classification, any applicable exemption and its basis, and actual notice/record dates are UNVERIFIED. EPA Region 7 administers Iowa's federal RCRA program; Iowa DNR independently confirms this. The Army is named emergency permittee; EPA's December 2024 enforcement release identifies American Ordnance as operator. Those facts do not establish which entity made the specific waste determination or bears financial liability. EPA's response document index still lists eight entries; no newly validated measurements retrieved.

Unsent records scope: request from Army and separately EPA Region 7 the dated non-sensitive classification decision(s), responsible office, legal basis for any exemption or status change, regulator receipt dates, supporting retention schedule, and aggregate post-treatment waste classification and disposal accounting. Request only releasable administrative records, excluding operational storage details, personal information and security-sensitive materials. Obtain issuer and recipient copies; neither a procurement notice nor a public permit substitutes for the underlying determination.
