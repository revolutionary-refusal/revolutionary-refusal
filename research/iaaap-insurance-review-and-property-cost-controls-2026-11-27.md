## Property cost controls
The current published PGI Part 245 edition, effective 7 May 2026, specifies one accountable contract at a time for government property, while permitting cross-contract use if authorized. Section 245.103-71 prescribes SF 30 for transfers of accountability between existing contracts. Section 245.103-70 requires documented acquisition justification, including environmental disposal costs in the overall-benefit assessment; exceptions and applicability must be checked against the actual contract and effective version at award. The Army's permit status does not alone establish the property accounting classification or contractual allocation of liabilities.
