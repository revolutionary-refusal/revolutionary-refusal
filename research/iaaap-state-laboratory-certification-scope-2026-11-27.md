# IAAAP — Iowa laboratory certification scope
27 November 2026

Iowa DNR's certified laboratory list generated 27 November 2026 identifies the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant Chemistry Lab as laboratory 005, classified Industrial/Private. The separate detail record lists certification from 24 January 2026 to 1 June 2027 for nonpotable water and solid waste/contaminated sites (water and soil/sludge). Drinking-water certification is not listed. Iowa DNR explains that certification is specific to analyte/method pairs, that the University of Iowa State Hygienic Laboratory assists with audits, and that EPA Region 7 administers Iowa's RCRA program.

The state certificate index lists an update dated 24 January 2026 and a certificate dated 16 May 2025. Attempts to retrieve the two attachments returned HTTP 400 unsupported multipart/form-data. Thus lead-specific method coverage, any accreditation required by the emergency permit, and actual incident sample assignments remain unknown. This is an identified laboratory, not evidence that it processed incident samples; a state certification is not independent validation of a particular result. No private information included.
