# IAAAP — laboratory procurement and data custody
26 November 2026 | Public environmental accountability.

EPA's November 26 release identifies federal regional, mobile and contracted private laboratories receiving incident samples. Its response register still lists eight documents; no validated post-event lab report was retrieved in this review. EPA's expectation of first results as early as November 27 is not a guarantee of publication.

New evidence route: EPA's Environmental Response Laboratory Network (ERLN) membership and tools pages, both updated December 5, 2025, describe optional basic ordering agreements for direct emergency funding; incident-specific task orders govern methods and reporting. The limited-access laboratory compendium (CETL) holds capability records; MyEXES is a centralized analytical submission and assessment system with inter-network exchange compatibility. Region 7's February 24, 2026 quality page describes its quality office's project approval and external-partner assessment responsibilities and regional laboratory accreditation for over 100 contaminants. Neither membership nor general accreditation proves the particular sample's analytical method was accredited or validated.

PROVEN: published program rules and incident lab categories. SUSPECTED: reliance on a central data exchange may create a preservation dependency if used here. UNVERIFIED: ERLN activation, MyEXES use, actual lab identity and task orders, method-specific accreditation, accepted results, costs and payments. Multiple labs do not automatically provide independent duplicate testing. Favorable preliminary air nondetections are counterevidence against a claim of demonstrated airborne contamination; they do not resolve other media. No wrongdoing established.

Unsent records scope: EPA Region 7 quality and contracting offices: whether ERLN/CETL/MyEXES were used, redacted laboratory award/order identifiers, aggregate obligations and actual payments separately, method-specific accreditation and quality acceptance summaries, aggregate result receipt/validation/publication timestamps, non-sensitive data export and revision histories. Seek separately held state/Army results and aggregate duplicate-sample comparisons; no private sample addresses or sensitive facility details. No request submitted. Derived note only, not original-source archival custody.
