# IAAAP — financial assurance scope, 26 November 2026

## New document reconciliation
The EPA-hosted September 2018 final standing hazardous-waste permit package (indexed as draft, but final-labeled main text) includes closure-plan attachments. Container-storage plan section J-3 (printed J-13) invokes the federal financial-requirements exemption. The separate OB/OD closure plan sections J-8 through J-12 (printed J-5) treat five categories of closure/post-closure estimates, assurance and liability requirements as inapplicable on federal-ownership grounds. This is site-specific documentary evidence of how the historical plan treated financial assurance, not merely a generic regulatory exemption. The text was retrieved; PDF screenshot request failed because the file was too large (199 MB). These attachment provisions are not independently established as the operative terms of the 2026 emergency permit.

40 CFR 264.140(c) exempts state and federal governments from Subpart H. Standing permit section IX.A also describes separate corrective-action financial responsibility and RCRA/CERCLA coordination. The November 13, 2026 emergency permit names the Army, whereas the 2018 standing permit names both the Army and American Ordnance. The 2024 EPA settlement announcement independently confirms the owner/operator distinction and reports corrective steps. None of this establishes a blanket exemption from cleanup duties, contractor contractual liability, or an insurance instrument covering the current incident. The September 18, 2026 financial-responsibility submission and subsequent correspondence remain the decisive missing records.
