# IAAAP — financial record custody and preservation
26 November 2026

EPA response register 17211 remains at eight listed documents; latest profile entry November 26. No new validated environmental result retrieved. Prior award/account discrepancy remains unresolved.

NARA current schedule index (reviewed November 25, 2026) links April 2020 GRS 1.1, pp. 1–2 visually checked. Item 010 (DAA-GRS-2013-0003-0001) provides six years from final payment or cancellation for official agency financial transaction records, with longer retention permitted for business needs. Item 011 covers reference copies, which can be disposed of after business use ends. The schedule expressly excludes contractor-maintained records and reporting copies sent to Treasury or OMB, which have separate authorities. FAR 4.703 generally provides contractor audit access for three years following final payment or the applicable specific period if earlier, subject to exceptions. NARA separately distinguishes holds for agency-held records from freezes for records in federal records centers. Actual schedules, cutoff dates and holds for this incident remain unknown. Neither an inactive solicitation nor permit expiry establishes a destruction date. This is a custody map, not an allegation or archival copy.
