# IAAAP — property access, sampling and claims reconciliation
2026-11-25 | Public accountability; not a safety advisory or legal advice.

## New evidence
- IAI-2026-11-25-RE1: The Army's two-page environmental right-of-entry template, listed on Army.mil under November 20 and published on DVIDS November 23, provides a maximum 36-month term from signing, subject to earlier remediation completion or recorded termination. Clause 1 grants irrevocable, assignable access for environmental investigation and remediation, including contractors. Clause 5 requires advance contact one day before entry; clause 6 requires written notice of land transfer. Both pages visually inspected. Blank template only; no evidence of execution, compulsory signature or actual entry.
- RE2: Clause 4 provides for repair or settlement at government discretion for damage caused in exercising the access right, capped by pre-damage fair market value of the fee interest and subject to available appropriations. It expressly preserves other applicable claims for damages outside that clause. This is not a blanket release of claims or a guaranteed payment. No signed agreement, incurred damage, valuation or appropriation verified.
- RE3: The November 24 Army claims guide states that claims generally must be received within two years of accrual, specifies a definite dollar amount and distinguishes final denial from six months of agency inaction. It also says that incomplete supporting records alone should not delay timely filing. Both pages visually inspected. This is important counterevidence to the previously identified state guidance using the incident date. Actual accrual and eligibility depend on facts and governing law.
- RE4: EPA response site 17211 lists a separate November 24 sampling-request channel. Whether that channel requires the Army's broader access instrument, whether limited sampling consent is available, and how tenants and out-of-state owners are handled have not been established. EPA permit II.L.4 requires the Army's analytical data to meet the quality objectives in the submitted quality assurance plan (page 6 visually inspected). Preliminary airborne nondetection previously recorded remains favorable evidence, not validated final multi-medium results. The two public access routes should not be conflated.
