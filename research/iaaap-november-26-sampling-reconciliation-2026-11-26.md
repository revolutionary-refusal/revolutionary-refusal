# IAAAP environmental sampling reconciliation — 2026-11-26

EPA's November 26 release reports 1,591 pre-event samples, more than 1,000 post-event samples, and continued real-time airborne lead nondetections. The November 25 release reported 1,200 pre-event samples. The published counts differ by 391 (32.58% relative to 1,200); the later count is described as updated intake, not proof of additional field collection after the event or data error. EPA expects initial post-event laboratory results as early as November 27, not a guaranteed deadline. Soil and dust collection is expected to finish November 26, with continued air sampling and a second drinking-water round. More than 130 previously sampled structures were resampled. EPA Region 7, mobile labs and contracted private labs are processing samples. Counts are not validated concentrations or unique sampling locations.

Illinois's November 25 statement corroborates the preliminary nondetection report but relies on federal monitoring; it is not independent analytical confirmation. The state remains on standby pending final data. EPA response register 17211 lists eight documents; the separate public data dashboard returned no readable data in this review. Absence of results in that register is not evidence of withholding. No finding of contamination or complete absence of exposure is justified by the preliminary monitoring alone.
