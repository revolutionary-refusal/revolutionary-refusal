The state attributes additional mitigation and expanded monitoring to public objections and state actions. This is a documented state assertion, not an independently established causal finding. Preliminary federal air-monitor nondetection is favorable evidence, not final laboratory validation.
- FFA2: EPA's historical Federal Facility Agreement, administrative docket VII-F-90-0029, bears a September 21, 1990 filing stamp; signature page dates are September 12, 17 and 20, 1990. The cover/index labels it 1991. Section XXXVI makes effectiveness conditional on a separate EPA notice; exact effective date is not established by the filing stamp. Section II requires notice to EPA of contractor identities and assigned work upon selection, for work under the agreement; contractor breach of agreement terms is not an Army defense. This is not a finding that the 2026 contractors breached the agreement or that current emergency work falls within its scope.
- FFA3: Sections XIX.E and XX (printed p.53) require notification to EPA when off-property access agreements cannot be obtained for work under the agreement, and Army record retention throughout the agreement plus at least seven years after termination, with 60 days' notice to EPA before subsequent destruction. Section XXXV requires written EPA termination notice. The historical text supplies a separate preservation basis from the emergency RCRA permit. Current amendments, applicability and termination status need confirmation; no evidence of improper destruction.
- FFA4: 42 USC 9611(e)(3) restricts Superfund remedial funding at federal facilities, subject to exceptions. EPA explains that federal facility oversight/removal may be funded separately and Executive Order 12580 requires reimbursement for removals at the facility. The historical agreement section XXXIV anticipates a later reimbursement amendment. No actual 2026 funding source, reimbursement obligation for a particular expenditure, or payment established. Section 9620 generally subjects federal agencies to CERCLA with specified exceptions; financial assurance exceptions are not blanket cleanup immunity.
