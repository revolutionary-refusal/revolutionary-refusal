# IAAAP: Correction to PIKA competition approval (26 November 2026)

This note supersedes the procurement approval and publication citations in findings 2–3 of the [25 November checkpoint](./iaaap-competition-approval-and-monitoring-checkpoint-2026-11-25.md). The original remains intact. This correction does not change the environmental monitoring findings.

**Approval.** On the reported 2 July 2026 award date, DoD Class Deviation 2026-O0017, Revision 1, directed use of DFARS 206.104-72, Table 206-1, instead of the general FAR approval table. The ordinary approving official is the contracting officer for a proposed value of $10 million or less, including options. The competition advocate tier begins above $10 million. If the reported $9,999,999 was the proposed value including all options, it was $1 below the threshold. The reported $770,810 initial obligation is not the approval base. Neither figure establishes who approved award W519TC26CA049. The signed justification and approval, proposed value including options, and applicability of any special noncompetitive follow-on rule remain unverified.

**Publication.** Revised FAR 6.301 generally requires publication within 14 days of award, or within 30 days for urgency under FAR 6.103-2, and at least 30 days of public availability where publication is required. A security exception can apply. For a 2 July award, 16 July and 1 August are conditional dates, not findings about actual posting. HigherGov's competition field says urgency, but its commentary cites national security; GovTribe says only one source. Verify the official award reason code, signed justification and approval, and SAM.gov posting history or applicable exception. No missing approval, late publication, or improper award is established.
