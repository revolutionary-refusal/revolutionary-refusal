# IAAAP — FEMA response and interagency reimbursement custody
26 November 2026. Public accountability note, not a safety advisory.

## New evidence
- PROVEN as published: FEMA National Watch Center's 26 November 2026 daily operations briefing, PDF page 4, identifies emergency declaration FEMA-3661-EM-IA as approved 24 November. Its Iowa incident section is designated final unless significant developments occur; monitoring continues.
- FEMA's 25 November regional update reports 381 residences canvassed: 180 indicated shelter-in-place plans, 38 self-evacuation plans, and 163 did not respond. Sum = 381; respective shares 47.24%, 9.97%, 42.78%. These are plans or nonresponses, not confirmed movements, injuries, or an estimate of total population. Separately, 30 households sought hotline assistance; overlap with the canvass is unknown.
- FEMA identifies public-health, environmental, meteorological and engineering agency participation. This provides separate institutional custodians for records, not independent confirmation of EPA laboratory measurements.
- PROVEN: 44 CFR 206.8(b), (d)(1)–(5) distinguishes work performed under independent disaster authorities from reimbursable mission-assigned work; it requires final accounting, separate billing by mission assignment, contract-level cost listings where contracts form a significant portion of charges, and supporting records available for audit for three years after submission of final billing. The clock is not measured from 25 November 2026.
- UNVERIFIED: whether each participating agency worked under a reimbursable assignment, mission assignment identifiers, obligated amounts, actual expenditures, transfers, final billing, cost shares and contractor recoveries. No IAAAP-specific duplicate reimbursement established.
- EPA site 17211 still lists eight documents on 26 November; EPA's current press statement reports preliminary nondetections and expects initial post-event laboratory receipts as early as 27 November. FEMA repeats EPA findings, not independent laboratory verification.

## Unsent records request scopes
1. FEMA Region 7: declaration and signed assistance directives, mission assignment identifiers and amendments, estimated and actual obligations, eligible costs, reimbursements, deobligations, final billing dates and applicable federal/state cost shares for EM-3661-IA.
2. Army, EPA and other participating agencies: aggregate expenses and accounting crosswalk for each assignment, separate independent-authority costs, applicable contract invoices, reimbursement receipts and any recoveries. Exclude personal and sensitive operational details.
3. Iowa emergency management and National Guard: aggregate canvass follow-up dispositions and assistance counts, source methodology, costs, and federal/state reconciliation. Do not seek household identities or locations.

## QA
FEMA briefing page 4 visually inspected. The earlier state outreach plan used a different population and cannot be treated as an apples-to-apples increase. Percentages total 99.99% due to rounding. A final situation report does not equal financial closeout or completion of environmental sampling. Reimbursement is conditional, not automatic. Separate custodian ledgers and original signed documents are needed. Public-source inventory, not a copy of original PDF bytes. No submissions made.
