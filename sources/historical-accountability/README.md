## Operation Winter SHIELD (2026) — official source trail

- [FBI ten-action PDF (direct)](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/operation-winter-shield-slick.pdf) — official FBI-hosted document; PDF contents not yet independently transcribed.
- [FBI ten-action PDF landing page](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/operation-winter-shield-slick.pdf/view) — official page titled *Operation Winter SHIELD: Ten Actions to Improve Cyber Resilience*; identifies a 292 KB PDF.
- [FBI Seattle introduction video and transcript](https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/fbi-seattle-winter-shield-intro.mp4/view) — official video page; Mike Herrington describes launching the initiative and ten cybersecurity actions, including anti-phishing and third-party risk.
- [FBI ten key cyber defenses infographic](https://www.fbi.gov/image-repository/winter-shield-ten-key-cyber-defenses/view) — official image landing page.
- [Fox News interview with FBI Director Kash Patel, November 20, 2026](https://www.foxnews.com/video/6408630982112) — secondary news video; site description connects initiative with recent technology outages. Full interview not independently transcribed.

**Finding (2026-11-20):** The FBI's official pages authenticate the program name and published materials. They do **not** establish that the program was caused by a specific classified-network outage or that the outages were cyberattacks. Keep those as separate questions.

## Financial manipulation and enforcement

- [FBI / U.S. Attorney, *Fourteen Arrested for Market Manipulation Schemes* (February 14, 2013)](https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/losangeles/press-releases/2013/fourteen-arrested-for-market-manipulation-schemes-that-caused-thousands-of-investors-to-lose-more-than-30-million) — official archived announcement of two indictments, 15 charged defendants, 14 arrests, alleged losses exceeding $30 million affecting over 20,000 investors. Describes offshore and nominee accounts, misleading promotion, cross-trading, and joint FBI/IRS-CI investigation. **Status: contemporary prosecution allegations, not proof of conviction; later dispositions not checked.** Cross-reference: financial accountability, information manipulation, offshore flows.

## Federal criminal procedure — interpreting case records

- [DOJ Justice 101: Charging](https://www.justice.gov/usao/justice-101/charging) — official explanation of federal grand-jury indictments, formal charges, and venue. An indictment is a charge, not a conviction. Use this to interpret the 2013 FBI/IRS market-manipulation announcement.
- **Follow-up:** Locate the indictments, docket numbers, defendant-by-defendant dispositions, and any documented restitution or forfeiture. Do not treat alleged losses as adjudicated recovery amounts.

- [DOJ Find Your United States Attorney](https://www.justice.gov/usao/find-your-united-states-attorney) — official directory of federal prosecutorial districts, office contacts, and news. Use to locate the relevant U.S. Attorney's Office for a case; this directory does not establish indictment or disposition details.

- [Information Quality Guidelines](https://www.justice.gov/legalpolicies#iq) — DOJ describes objectivity, utility, integrity, and a correction-request process; follow the linked Information Quality page for actual procedure.
- [Other legal-policy sections](https://www.justice.gov/legalpolicies#other) — fragment supplied for preservation; page also addresses external links, public-domain status, citations, seals, and third-party materials. Fragment target may not correspond to a distinct heading.

**Audit note:** These are four entry URLs for one DOJ page, not four independent sources. Government publication authenticates the agency's statement, not necessarily every claim within it. Avoid reproducing protected seals and third-party content without checking restrictions.
