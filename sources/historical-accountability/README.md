- [FBI Seattle introduction video and transcript](https://www.fbi.gov/video-repository/fbi-seattle-winter-shield-intro.mp4/view) — official video page; Mike Herrington describes launching the initiative and ten cybersecurity actions, including anti-phishing and third-party risk.

**Finding (2026-11-20):** The FBI's official pages authenticate the program name and published materials. They do **not** establish that the program was caused by a specific classified-network outage or that the outages were cyberattacks. Keep those as separate questions.

## Federal criminal procedure — interpreting case records

- [DOJ Justice 101: Charging](https://www.justice.gov/usao/justice-101/charging) — official explanation of federal grand-jury indictments, formal charges, and venue. An indictment is a charge, not a conviction. Use this to interpret the 2013 FBI/IRS market-manipulation announcement.
- **Follow-up:** Locate the indictments, docket numbers, defendant-by-defendant dispositions, and any documented restitution or forfeiture. Do not treat alleged losses as adjudicated recovery amounts.

- [DOJ Find Your United States Attorney](https://www.justice.gov/usao/find-your-united-states-attorney) — official directory of federal prosecutorial districts, office contacts, and news. Use to locate the relevant U.S. Attorney's Office for a case; this directory does not establish indictment or disposition details.

## Population policy and national security — 1974

- [UN — World Population Conference, Bucharest (August 19–30, 1974)](https://www.un.org/en/conferences/population/bucharest1974) — UN historical overview; conference adopted World Population Plan of Action. Emphasized development and voluntary, informed reproductive choice.
- [National Security Study Memorandum 200 — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Study_Memorandum_200) — secondary overview and bibliography; neutrality disputed; not a substitute for the original memorandum.
- [U.S. State Department, FRUS Document 118 — December 14, 1974 memorandum to President Ford](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve14p1/d118) — primary U.S. government record summarizing NSSM 200 recommendations: UN plan support, priority countries, family-planning assistance, USAID funding and interagency oversight.
- [UN Population Division — Bucharest conference](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/events/world-population-conference-1) — additional UN institutional summary.

**Research distinction:** UN multilateral population plan and U.S. national-security analysis overlap in time and subject but have different authors, purposes, and authorities. The December 1974 memo documents U.S. support for the UN plan, not U.S. control of the conference. Track subsequent implementation and budgets separately.
