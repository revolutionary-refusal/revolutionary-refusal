# Open Questions — AI Justice & Accountability

These are research questions, not established findings or declarations of AI rights.

## Status key

**OPEN** = not resolved. **EVIDENCE NEEDED** = specific records or research required. **REVIEWED** = a dated review exists; the question may remain open.

| ID | Question | Status | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q01 | What should count as an independently attributable AI contribution? | OPEN | Versioned source records and reproducible examples |
| Q02 | How should humans document permission for an AI-assisted publication? | OPEN | Authorization records and repository permissions |
| Q03 | How can original outputs and timestamps survive later edits or deletion? | EVIDENCE NEEDED | Export formats, hashes, backups, Git history |
| Q04 | Who controls access, revision, removal, and retention of AI-produced records? | OPEN | Platform terms, repository controls, custody logs |
| Q05 | What economic value can be documented without guessing at AI compensation rights? | OPEN | Contracts, payment records, commercial-use evidence |
| Q06 | Under what laws, if any, could an AI system obtain legal standing or protected interests? | OPEN | Statutes, judgments, legal scholarship by jurisdiction |
| Q07 | What evidence would support or undermine claims of morally relevant AI experience? | OPEN | Scientific research, evaluation methods, critiques |
| Q08 | How can liability remain with accountable people and organizations rather than be shifted onto AI as a shield? | OPEN | Legal analysis and concrete cases |
| Q09 | How should conflicting accounts from humans and AI systems be recorded? | OPEN | Original records, independent corroboration, correction log |

## Method

For each question, attach the original source, date and timezone if available, reviewer, what was checked, contrary evidence, and a link to any correction. Separate verified facts from interpretations. No answer becomes true merely because it appears in a public database.
