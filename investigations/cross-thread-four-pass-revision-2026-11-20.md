## Critical corrections to earlier assistant statements
- **No independent proof of a hidden Treasury 'auto-balancing' software bug** was produced. GAO establishes material weaknesses in intragovernmental accounting and consolidation, not that cash vanished. GAO-26-108073: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108073
- **The $1 quintillion USAspending subaward is a data-quality anomaly**, explicitly documented by GAO-26-109100 (PDF p.18–19), not a real payment or newly created money. Five other implausibly large subaward entries are noted. https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-109100.pdf
- **GAO-26-109100:** 20 selected programs represent about **$1.1 trillion in FY2025 obligations**; broader state/local assistance is estimated around $1.2 trillion. These are not interchangeable totals. Five of 20 had documented fraud risk identification and likelihood assessment; 15 lacked both documented elements. 22 open recommendations relate to reviewed programs. The $233–521bn annual estimated fraud loss uses **FY2018–22 government-wide data**, not a finding of losses in the selected 20 programs.
- **GAO-23-105700 DOJ reporting deficiency was remedied**: GAO marks the DOJ recommendation 'Closed – Implemented' after FY2023 reporting resumed. It is inaccurate to imply the 2019–22 omission necessarily persists. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105700
- **Grant accounting:** awarded ≠ obligated ≠ drawn ≠ spent ≠ retained; expired ≠ stolen; 'resolved' OIG recommendation ≠ 'closed'; a deobligation is not necessarily a cash refund. Program-specific terms control.
- **Pantego arithmetic:** $94,200 + $5,000 + $4,000 + $3,400 = $106,600, exceeding the reported $100,000 receipt by $6,600. These figures were reported earlier in this thread; no invoice-to-payment reconciliation was completed. **Do not call $6,600 a misuse or 'unspent' balance.**
- **Smith County:** the prior estimate of $1.58m 'unreconciled' subtracts identified *categories* ($619k) from reported *funding* (~$2.2m), which may have different timing and bases. It is **not a verified missing balance**.
- **FBI page-count arithmetic:** 82,039 + 2,080 + 83 − 13 + 13 + 44 = **84,246**. The correct total does not prove that all source documents were inventoried or released. The underlying 13MB DOJ memorandum was not fully retrievable in this audit pass; detailed entries remain attributed to prior thread research.
- **Epstein records:** serials 293, 402, 407, 411, 538, 684; EFTA01731021; case IDs 50D-NY-3027571 and 31E-MM-108062 are **leads requiring original-page checks and release mapping**. The reported '31E-MM-106062' and other variants must not be silently normalized as the same case. Serial number, Bates page number, filename, and file hash are separate identifiers.

CBS also identifies potential de-duplication of email chains and an estimated 1.2m R1 numbering gaps; **these must not be added to page-count deficits as independent missing documents**. https://www.cbsnews.com/news/epstein-files-classified-material-missing-documents-suggest/

## Reconciliation Watchdog — design brief retained
Read-only, offline-first, deterministic checker for systems that cannot directly communicate. Inputs: independent exports (CSV, fixed-width, PDF inventories, logs), source identifiers, signed timestamps, and hashes. Outputs: matched records, unmatched exceptions, authorized corrections with rationale, versioned evidence, and independent human review. **Never auto-clear an unexplained exception.** Operator competency and automation bias are explicit control tests. This is a concept, not an implemented machine.
