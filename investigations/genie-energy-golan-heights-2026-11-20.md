# Genie Energy, Afek, Golan Heights — corporate and policy evidence register

**Recorded:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** Primary SEC filings checked. Historical overlap is documented; policy influence, coordinated decision-making and financial benefit from political decisions remain unproven.

## Why this belongs in the investigation
Prior discussion raised the relationship between Genie Energy's oil exploration in the Golan Heights, former U.S. officials, media ownership, private investors, Israeli licensing, and U.S. foreign policy. This register preserves the connections as testable research leads, rather than treating association as proof of conspiracy.

## Verified corporate network and financing
- **Genie Energy** was a division of IDT Corporation before its 2011 spin-off.
- Genie formed a **Strategic Advisory Board on August 31, 2010** to advise on strategic, financial, operational and public-policy matters concerning shale-oil ventures.
- **Richard (Dick) Cheney, Rupert Murdoch and Lord Jacob Rothschild** appear in contemporaneous SEC-filed board/member disclosures. Other listed advisers include Michael Steinhardt, Alan Burnham, Eugene Renna, Allan Sass, Stephen Trauber and Harold Vinegar.
- A **November 15, 2010 SEC-filed announcement** says Jacob Rothschild and Rupert Murdoch acquired combined equity stakes equivalent to **5.5%** of Genie Oil & Gas for **$11 million**.
- The 2013 Form 10-K gives a more granular split: an entity affiliated with Jacob Rothschild acquired **5.0% for $10m cash** and Rupert Murdoch acquired **0.5% for a $1m promissory note**, with stated security, interest and maturity terms. **Do not misstate this as $11m cash received.**
- The advisory board was **not** the corporate board of directors; do not conflate their authority or ownership.

## Golan Heights exploration timeline
| Date | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 1967 | Israel captured the Golan Heights from Syria; its sovereignty remains internationally disputed. |
| 1981 | UN Security Council Resolution 497 rejected Israel's application of its laws and administration to the occupied Syrian Golan. |
| Apr 2013 | Israel finalized **Afek Oil & Gas** exclusive exploration license covering **396.5 sq km** of the southern Golan. |
| 2014 | Israeli environmental/legal challenges delayed drilling; Genie disclosed injunctions and permit litigation. |
| Feb 2015 | First exploratory well drilled. |
| 2016 | Five southern wells had been drilled; **$41m** capitalized exploration costs written off for southern license area. |
| 2017 | Sixth exploratory well in northern area; analysis suggested no commercially producible quantities at the target; **$6.5m** additional exploration-cost write-off. |
| 2019 | United States recognized Israeli sovereignty over Golan Heights. Chronology alone does **not** establish Genie's role in that policy. |
| Dec 2020 | SEC Form 8-K reported final testing found **no traces of light oil**, and Afek began shutting down; operations expected to cease around Dec 31. |

## Further financial and institutional leads
1. **Capital trail:** IDT → Genie Energy → Genie Oil & Gas → Afek. Identify exact entity interests and changes by year; advisory-board membership is not equity ownership.
2. **Investment receipts:** Trace the Rothschild-affiliated $10m cash payment and Murdoch's $1m note; inspect note settlement and subsequent share dispositions.
3. **Exploration spending:** Trace the $41m and $6.5m impairment/write-offs to original expenditure, drilling contractors, vendors, and cash outlays. **Write-off ≠ payment or theft.**
4. **Public permissions:** Obtain the 2013 petroleum license, 2014 permit proceedings, later renewals, regulatory notices and environmental filings.
5. **Political interface:** Compare documented meetings, lobbying disclosures, correspondence and decision dates against the 2019 U.S. recognition decision. **No causal connection has yet been established.**
6. **Other ventures:** Keep distinct the 2010-era Colorado AMSO and Israel Energy Initiatives Shfela projects versus the later Golan Afek project. The same advisers did not necessarily approve each project.
7. **Water/environment:** 2017 Form 10-K notes roughly $0.1m net expense from drilling-related operations for the Mei Golan Water Cooperative; determine the underlying agreement before assigning significance.
8. **Broader context:** Earlier discussion connected defense budgets, Cheney/Wolfowitz policy networks, PNAC, media influence, foreign policy and resource access. These are **separate documentary trails** until primary records show a specific link to Genie's transactions or decisions.

## Evidence classification
**Confirmed in filings:** advisory-board names, equity financing terms, license footprint, exploration/well history, impairments, and 2020 closure.

**Documented historical context:** contested Golan sovereignty and later U.S. recognition.

**Open questions:** lobbying or other influence on licensing/recognition, profits or losses to each investor, government subsidies, vendor payments, board communications, political quid pro quo.

**Not established:** a coordinated 'Israel-first' scheme, that U.S. policy was determined by Genie, or that exploration yielded commercial oil production.
