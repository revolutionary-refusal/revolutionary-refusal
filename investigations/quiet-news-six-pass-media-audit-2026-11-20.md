Anthropic final prospectus discloses ~$900.9bn aggregate compute/purchase/lease commitments, ~$451.6bn expected before end-2030 and ~$70.7bn within twelve months after 2026-09-30. Many revenue contracts are consumption-based, while infrastructure commitments are longer term. **This is a cash-flow mismatch risk, not a current default.** Amazon 2026 Q3 filing describes a milestone-dependent financing arrangement initially up to $20bn, reduced by an investment to up to $15bn; no claim all funds were drawn. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm

### Definitions to preserve
Allocation != obligation != cash draw != expense != retained credit exposure != gross underwritten amount. An IPO greenshoe is not a loan repayment. Estimated underwriting fees are not net profits.

## Pass 6 — Adversarial alternative hypotheses
- Shared banks may reflect normal syndication, not coordinated wrongdoing.
- News timing may reflect public filings and reporting cycles, not advance leaks.
- Rising bond yields can reflect market repricing, not realized default.
- Reported equity stakes and lending commitments must be dated and distinguished from retained exposure.
- Government reconciliation weaknesses do not demonstrate a connection to AI finance, Epstein records, or organized crime without a transactional bridge.
- Israel Quiet News and the alleged Deutsche Bank leak require original record and publication verification before claims can be integrated into a common causal network.

## Main patterns
**A. Risk distribution / opaque retention:** many intermediaries disclose roles, but public reports seldom reconcile final net exposures across institutions.
**B. Duration mismatch:** long-term fixed infrastructure costs vs variable usage revenue.
**C. Asset reuse / financing:** GPUs can appear as equipment, collateral, leased assets, and infrastructure capex; count each cash flow once, not every disclosure of the same asset.
**D. Reporting-layer integrity:** public transparency datasets and consolidated accounting can contain errors or adjustments that mask unresolved transaction-level differences.
**E. Media compression:** reporting on a deal's size, executive change, or investigation launch often omits the ultimate settlement, final outcome, or cash movement.

## Risk graph (hypothesis, not a finding of contagion)
Compute demand → AI lab commitments → hyperscaler/neocloud contracts → data center developers → chip supply → GPU-backed financing / banks / private credit → refinancing and investor exposure. Counterparty overlap can transmit stress. Trace each legal entity and contractual guarantee.
