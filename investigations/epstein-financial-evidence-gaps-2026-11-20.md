# Epstein document reconciliation — financial evidence gaps
Date: 2026-11-20
Status: External investigative finding corroborated at source; original check images not yet independently matched.

## New lead: missing check images
CBS News reports that a sequence of approximately 4,500 released images of checks from Epstein accounts contains 66 gaps in the prior numbering system; sometimes a check front/back, sometimes both. CBS also reports tens of thousands of gaps when comparing earlier discovery Bates numbers against EFTA release numbering. These are evidence of gaps in the published sequences, not independently established destruction or intent.
Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/epstein-files-classified-material-missing-documents-suggest/

## Transaction-level matching required
For each of the reported 66 check-image gaps:
- earlier Bates identifier(s)
- EFTA preceding/following identifiers
- check front/back designation
- whether alternate release exists
- original source page and hash
- lawful withholding or duplication explanation, if documented

Do not publish victim personal information or unredacted bank account identifiers.

## Findings and limits
- Documented by original FBI memorandum: reconciliation changes and initial inaccessibility of serials.
- Reported by CBS: 66 missing check images in a released sequence.
- Not yet independently verified: exact 66 identifiers, current restoration status, or transaction amounts.
