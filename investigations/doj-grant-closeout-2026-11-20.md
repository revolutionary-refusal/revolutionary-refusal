## Question

## Governing distinctions
- **2 CFR 200.344:** closeout and treatment of unobligated balances, subject to award terms and applicable revisions. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/subtitle-A/chapter-II/part-200/subpart-D/section-200.344
- **GAO-12-704T:** historical expired-grant balance oversight. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-12-704t
- **GAO-23-105700:** DOJ reporting of expired-grant balances; check corrective action and implementation dates. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105700
- **Indiana University residual balances:** fixed-price residuals may be retained under specific conditions, not universally refundable. https://research.iu.edu/awards-agreements/award-closeout/residual-balances.html
- **30 CFR 885.19 and 886.20:** program-specific reclamation rules, **not** universal DOJ rules. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/chapter-VII/subchapter-R/part-885/section-885.19 ; https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/chapter-VII/subchapter-R/part-886/section-886.20
- CRS document supplied for follow-up: https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48243/R48243.2.pdf
